# Unified Marketing Measurement System

## Objective

This repository documents my learning and build journey to create a causal, decision-ready marketing measurement system.

The goal is to move from correlation-based attribution to a more reliable measurement framework that combines:

- Marketing data architecture
- Attribution diagnostics
- Incrementality testing
- Geo Lift testing
- Marketing Mix Modeling
- Lead quality and LTV prediction
- AI-assisted measurement interpretation
- Budget decision logic

## Why This Project Exists

Traditional marketing reporting often over-relies on attribution and platform-reported ROAS. These methods are useful for diagnostics, but they do not always prove whether marketing caused incremental business growth.

This project is designed to build a practical system that answers better questions:

- Which channels are truly incremental?
- Which campaigns create high-quality leads?
- Where should budget increase, decrease, or be tested further?
- When should we trust attribution, experiments, MMM, or LTV models?
- How can AI help explain measurement evidence and support better decisions?

## Measurement Philosophy

Attribution explains customer journeys.

Incrementality testing proves causal lift.

Geo Lift helps measure media impact where user-level randomization is difficult.

MMM supports cross-channel budget allocation and long-term planning.

Lead quality and LTV models ensure marketing is optimized for business value, not just volume.

SQL and dbt provide the trusted data foundation.

AI acts as a copilot layer that helps interpret evidence, explain trade-offs, design tests, summarize results, and document decisions.

## Final Product Vision

The final product will be a prototype Unified Marketing Measurement Copilot.

It will combine data, models, experiments, and AI-assisted decision logic to recommend:

- Increase budget
- Hold budget
- Reduce budget
- Test further
- Investigate data quality
- Improve targeting
