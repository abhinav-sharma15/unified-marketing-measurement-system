# Market Landscape

## Purpose

This document tracks the market landscape for unified marketing measurement products.

The goal is to understand what already exists, where commercial tools are strong, and where an internal prototype can add value.

## Product Categories

### 1. Unified Marketing Measurement Platforms

These platforms combine MMM, attribution, incrementality, budget optimization, and decision support.

Examples to study:

- Lifesight
- Sellforte
- Roivenue
- Measured
- Haus
- LiftLab
- Rockerbox
- Triple Whale
- Northbeam

### 2. MMM Platforms

These platforms focus on marketing mix modeling, response curves, saturation, ROI, marginal ROI, and budget optimization.

Examples to study:

- Google Meridian
- Meta Robyn
- Recast
- PyMC-Marketing
- Analytic Partners
- Nielsen
- Kantar

### 3. Incrementality Platforms

These platforms focus on causal measurement through experiments, geo tests, conversion lift, holdouts, and synthetic controls.

Examples to study:

- Measured
- Haus
- LiftLab
- AppsFlyer
- Incremental

### 4. Attribution Platforms

These platforms focus on journey analytics, first-party tracking, multi-touch attribution, campaign attribution, and ROAS reporting.

Examples to study:

- Rockerbox
- Northbeam
- Triple Whale
- AppsFlyer
- Adobe Analytics
- GA4
- Salesforce attribution tools
- HubSpot attribution tools

## What the Market Already Solves Well

- MMM modeling
- Attribution reporting
- Geo Lift testing
- Budget scenario planning
- Incrementality measurement
- Ecommerce attribution
- Campaign performance dashboards

## Common Market Gaps

- Clean internal marketing data architecture
- B2B lead quality and LTV integration
- Measurement governance
- Experiment memory and reusable evidence base
- Clear rules for which measurement method to trust
- Integration with existing internal reporting stacks
- Explainability for senior stakeholders
- Lightweight decision logic for internal teams

## Product Opportunity

The goal of this project is not to build a full commercial MMM or attribution platform.

The opportunity is to build a practical internal measurement operating system that connects:

- SQL/dbt marketing data
- Attribution diagnostics
- Incrementality evidence
- Geo Lift results
- MMM outputs
- Lead quality and LTV signals
- AI-assisted interpretation
- Decision recommendations

## Working Positioning

A lightweight internal Unified Marketing Measurement Copilot that helps marketing, analytics, and business stakeholders decide which channels are truly incremental, which campaigns create quality demand, and where budget should increase, decrease, or be tested further.
