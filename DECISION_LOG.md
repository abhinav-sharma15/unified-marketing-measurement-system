## Decision 001: Project Direction

### Date

2026-07-31

### Decision

Build a GitHub-managed Unified Marketing Measurement System as a practical learning and product prototype.

### Rationale

Marketing measurement often relies too heavily on attribution and platform-reported ROAS. The project aims to create a more robust system that combines attribution diagnostics, causal testing, MMM, LTV prediction, and AI-assisted decision support.

### Implication

The project will focus on both learning and usable product development. Each week should produce a concrete artifact such as documentation, SQL, mock data, Python code, notebook, or decision framework.

## Decision 002: Measurement Philosophy

### Date

2026-07-31

### Decision

Use attribution for diagnostics, incrementality for causal proof, MMM for budget planning, LTV for value optimization, and AI for interpretation and governance.

### Rationale

No single measurement method answers every business question. A unified system should triangulate evidence rather than force all decisions through one model.

### Implication

The final product should produce decision recommendations with evidence summaries, confidence levels, and suggested next actions.
